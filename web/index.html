Click + Add course or any dashed choose tile to pick the real course for that requirement — the picker shows when each course actually runs (from STAR) and whether its prerequisites are in place. Drag a course to another semester to move it, click it for details, use Gap on a semester you sit out, and press Check rules to see whether the schedule is likely to work. Set Start term to the semester you began: semesters before the current term count as completed, and completed courses are shared with the prereq map's “taken” list in both directions.
